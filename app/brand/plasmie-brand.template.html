Name

Why Plasmie

Plasmie

The name since 2026-10-04 (ADR 0014). Soft to say, sounds like a small living thing, and plasmie .com/.app/.ai were free. Plasmie is the species; in time each person names, colours and dresses their own. Where the name comes from is a secret the app keeps for anyone who looks closely.
