# Knowledge vault — maintenance contract

This is an **LLM-maintained knowledge wiki** (Karpathy's llm-wiki pattern). Read
this before touching anything. Your job is to keep the wiki accurate and
cross-linked; the human curates sources and asks questions.

## Three layers

| Layer | Path | Who owns it | Rule |
|-------|------|-------------|------|
| **Raw sources** | `raw/` | the hourly loop | **Transient staging.** The loop appends; you never hand-edit it. One subdir per source: `slack/ claude/ (Claude Code + AgentCLI sessions) outlook/ (optional) github/ git/ (local commits) confluence/ inbox/`. Dated activity dumps (`YYYY-MM-DD.md` in `slack/ claude/ outlook/ github/ git/`) are **pruned after ~14 days** — don't rely on old raw being present. `github/<repo>/` (backfill mirror) and `confluence/` (backfill mirrors) are rebuilt on demand, not pruned; `inbox/` is manual drops (`plasmie-capture.sh`). Durable knowledge = promoted wiki notes; durable record = `log.md`. |
| **Wiki** | `wiki/` | **you** (the LLM) | Curated, synthesized pages, **flat**: notes sit directly in `wiki/` (no subfolders — structure is `tags` plus generated maps). `wiki/mocs/` holds generated per-tag maps of content (never hand-edit); `wiki/attachments/` holds the PDFs/HTML/SVG that notes refer to. Create, merge, and cross-link notes. This is the answer layer — `ask` retrieves only from here. |
| **Meta** | `meta/` | tooling | Templates, promotion drafts (`promotion-drafts/`), digests, `coverage-gaps.md`, `improvement-loop.md`, `usage.tsv`. Not knowledge. |

Two root files are **generated — never hand-edit them**:
- `index.md` — catalog of every wiki page, regenerated by `reindex.py`.
- `log.md` — **the comprehensive, durable audit**: every ingest, promotion, and prune, append-only and never cleaned. Because `raw/` is transient, this is the accountability record. Grep: `grep '^## \[' log.md`.

`archive/` holds done/dead material. Retention of raw activity dumps is `PLASMIE_RAW_RETENTION_DAYS` (default 14).

## Frontmatter (every wiki note)

```yaml
---
type: note | gotcha | project | reference | decision | concept | pattern | person | convention | clipping | index | moc
title: Human Title
description: one line — this is what shows in the catalog and drives retrieval
tags: [kebab-case, ...]
source: where it came from (optional; promote-apply fills it for promoted notes)
timestamp: 2026-07-02T00:00:00Z
---
```

The type list is descriptive, not aspirational — it matches what the vault
actually contains. `gotcha` (a trap and its escape), `decision` (a choice and
its reason) and `pattern` (a shape that recurs) are load-bearing: together they
are a third of the wiki. `clipping` is imported external material; `index` is a hand-curated hub note.
`moc` pages are generated by `reindex.py` into `wiki/mocs/` — never hand-write one.

`title` + `description` are high-signal: they populate `index.md` and are what
qmd embeds. Write them well.

## Links

- Cross-reference with `[[wikilinks]]` (Obsidian resolves by basename, so they
  survive file moves). Use markdown `[text](relative/path.md)` only for
  same-directory links.
- Link liberally. A page's value is its connections. When you add a fact to one
  page, link the entities and concepts it touches.

## Operations

**Ingest** (a new raw file appeared): read it, extract durable facts, and
update/create the relevant `wiki/` pages — then link them. A single source may
touch several wiki pages. The hourly loop does the *capture*; promotion into the
wiki is nightly: `promote.sh` (on-device map-reduce drafts) → `dedupe.py` → `promote-apply.py --auto`
(confident items filed into `wiki/`, borderline ones held as `*.hold.md` for human review
in the menu-bar app, or `promote-apply.py <draft> --apply`).

**Query**: retrieve with **qmd** (hybrid BM25 + vector + rerank), never grep:
```
qmd query "your question" --json        # ranked hits across raw + wiki
qmd query "..." -c wiki                  # restrict to the curated layer
qmd get wiki/<note>.md                   # read a specific page
```
A good answer worth keeping should be **filed back** as a new wiki page — that's
how the wiki compounds instead of evaporating into chat.

**Lint** (periodic): `reindex.py` regenerates the catalog and flags dead links;
`plasmie-improve.py` archives resolved drafts, **prunes stale raw dumps**, and logs
changes. Also watch for contradictions, stale claims, and concepts with no
inbound links.

## Boundaries

- Don't hand-edit or hand-delete `raw/` — the maintenance loop owns it (appends,
  then prunes on retention). What actually happened is preserved in `log.md`, not
  in old raw files. So **promote durable facts into `wiki/` before they age out**.
- Work content only (laptop): no personal finances, family, or health.
- Don't hand-edit `index.md` / `log.md`; change the generators instead.

## Goal context

Machinery lives in `~/.plasmie` (ingest, promote, improve, reindex,
dedupe, doctor, ask + their tests). This repo is data only. `VAULT_DIR` in
`config.env` points here. Never hand-edit `raw/`, `index.md` or `log.md`.
Each connector has an On/Off switch (app: Settings → Connectors, or
`PLASMIE_DISABLED_SOURCES="github,confluence"` in `config.env`): Off sources are skipped
by ingest and not backfilled when re-enabled, so an empty `raw/<source>/` can mean "off".
